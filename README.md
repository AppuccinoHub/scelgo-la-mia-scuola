# Scelgo la mia scuola

Italian 2 guided project: students pick one Italian high school in Rome, then tell the story in 5 recorded slides.

- `index.html`: the whole app (one file, original art and fonts embedded). Open it in Chrome. Paths A, B and C are picked on the first screen, or with `?path=A|B|C`.
- Teacher tools: add `?teacher=1`.
- No logins. No names. Answers stay in the browser on that computer. Recordings stay in the open tab until saved.
