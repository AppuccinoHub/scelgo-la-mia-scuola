# Scelgo la mia scuola

**Live links**
- **App:** https://appuccinohub.github.io/scelgo-la-mia-scuola/
- **Printable files and downloads page:** https://appuccinohub.github.io/scelgo-la-mia-scuola/downloads.html
- **Offline app (one HTML file):** https://appuccinohub.github.io/scelgo-la-mia-scuola/Scelgo-la-mia-scuola.html

Italian 2 guided project: students pick one Italian high school in Rome, then tell the story in 5 recorded slides.

- `index.html`: the whole app (one file, original art and fonts embedded). Open it in Chrome. Paths A, B and C are picked on the first screen, or with `?path=A|B|C`.
- `Scelgo-la-mia-scuola.html`: the same app as a download, for use with no internet (download it, then open it from the Files app).
- `downloads.html`: student page with the printable PDFs in `pdf/` (Path A, B and C workbooks, School files, What matters to me, and a print packet).
- Pick step: 4 cards, plus a **None of these? See all school types** button that opens all 17 types (each with real Rome school files). Any type can be picked, from the cards or from the "What matters to me" matcher.
- School files: 54 real Rome schools (statali, paritarie and IeFP centers), built into the app so no outside websites are needed.
- No logins. No names. Answers stay in the browser on that computer. Recordings stay in the open tab until saved.
